# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

This documentation describes how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and improves projects. Use these guides as the shared operating handbook for onboarding, project coordination, and day-to-day delivery.

## Process overview

OctoAcme projects begin by initiating and validating the need, defining measurable success criteria, and aligning stakeholders on the expected outcome. Approved work moves into planning, where teams prioritize a backlog, estimate scope, identify dependencies and risks, agree on milestones and a release plan, and document the Definition of Done.

Delivery is iterative. The team uses a project board and regular delivery rhythms to build, test, review, and track progress. PM and Product Manager alignment keeps scope, timeline, and priorities transparent, while developers implement and test changes and QA/Testing validates acceptance criteria and quality. Communication is recurring and role-based, including team standups, delivery or planning syncs, stakeholder updates, and documented escalations.

Risk and dependency management is ongoing: risks are recorded with owners and mitigation plans, reviewed regularly, and escalated when needed. Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, smoke testing, release documentation, and rollback or mitigation readiness. After deployment, teams verify the result, communicate with stakeholders, and capture incidents or lessons in retrospectives. The learning cycle continues by turning retrospective findings into owned, dated backlog actions and measuring their impact.

## Core roles

- **Project Manager (PM)**: coordinates delivery, schedule, risks, dependencies, and communications.
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success.
- **Developers**: implement, review, test, and document solutions.
- **QA/Testing**: validate quality and acceptance criteria.
- **Stakeholders**: provide context, decisions, approvals, and feedback.
