# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This guide is the entry point to OctoAcme's project management processes. The documents below explain how teams validate and initiate work, plan delivery, execute and track progress, manage risk and communication, release changes, and improve through retrospectives.

## Project lifecycle

OctoAcme projects move through a connected lifecycle:

1. **Initiate and validate** the need, identify stakeholders, define measurable outcomes, and decide whether to proceed.
2. **Plan** scope, priorities, backlog, milestones, team responsibilities, dependencies, and risks.
3. **Execute iteratively** with small increments, regular coordination, testing, reviews, and progress tracking.
4. **Release and verify** changes with passing quality checks, prepared release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation readiness.
5. **Reflect and improve** by capturing lessons after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents and tracking follow-up actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications. Product Managers define outcomes and priorities. Developers build and test the solution; QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular team and stakeholder communication, active risk management, and clear ownership support the lifecycle throughout.
