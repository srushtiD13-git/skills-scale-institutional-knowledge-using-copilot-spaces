# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

The following personas are candidates that projects should apply based on scope and staffing needs. Roles may be combined or shared on smaller projects. Each role should have clear ownership for its responsibilities and explicit handoffs with the existing delivery roles.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers represent customer needs and translate them into usable, accessible, and testable experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and synthesize qualitative and quantitative user research
- Develop interaction flows, wireframes, prototypes, and visual designs
- Validate usability and accessibility requirements
- Maintain design decisions and handoff specifications
- Identify experience risks and unmet user needs

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer value
- Provide clear, consistent design direction
- Reduce implementation rework and design ambiguity

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Aligns with the Product Manager on problem statements, priorities, and research findings
- Collaborates with Developers on feasibility and implementation details
- Reviews acceptance criteria and usability scenarios with QA/Testing
- Shares findings and proposed experiences with Stakeholders
- Records unresolved design decisions for Project Manager follow-up

---

## Technical Lead or Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide engineering direction, technical quality, and architecture decisions across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate architecture and technical approaches
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration needs
- Support estimates and sequencing of technical work
- Review significant designs and implementation approaches
- Promote maintainability, security, scalability, and observability

### Goals
- Deliver a coherent and maintainable technical solution
- Surface technical trade-offs early
- Improve engineering reliability and delivery predictability

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Coordinates Developers and clarifies engineering expectations
- Advises the Project Manager on technical risks, dependencies, and delivery impacts
- Partners with the Product Manager on scope, feasibility, and trade-offs
- Works with UX/UI roles to ensure designs are technically feasible
- Works with QA/Testing on testability and with Security/Privacy roles on safeguards

---

## Data/Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure that project outcomes are measurable, instrumented, and supported by reliable evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement plans
- Define event instrumentation, dashboards, and analysis approaches
- Establish data-quality checks and reporting expectations
- Interpret results and communicate learnings
- Identify measurement gaps and risks

### Goals
- Provide reliable, actionable outcome data
- Improve data-informed decisions
- Measure the impact of delivered increments

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Partners with the Product Manager to define and validate success metrics
- Works with Developers to implement instrumentation and data pipelines
- Works with QA/Testing to validate tracking and reporting
- Reports outcomes and risks to the Project Manager and Stakeholders
- Shares findings with the broader team to support product decisions

---

## Release/Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness so deployments are controlled, observable, reversible, and communicated.

### Responsibilities
- Plan deployment and release windows
- Coordinate smoke tests, monitoring, rollback, and post-release verification
- Maintain operational readiness and incident response expectations
- Coordinate support and stakeholder communications
- Document operational risks and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and downtime
- Improve deployment and rollback reliability
- Ensure the team is prepared to operate released functionality

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing on release criteria and verification
- Coordinates timing, risk, and announcements with the Project Manager
- Provides operational and incident updates to Stakeholders
- Supplies operational feedback and constraints to the Product Manager
- Partners with Security/Privacy roles on incident and remediation planning

---

## Security/Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads provide specialist guidance to ensure project decisions meet security, privacy, and compliance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on applicable security and privacy requirements
- Review designs and implementations for material risks
- Coordinate threat, privacy, or compliance assessments
- Recommend controls and mitigation plans
- Support escalation and remediation for security or privacy issues

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk
- Ensure controls are traceable to requirements
- Support timely resolution of findings

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Partners with the Technical Lead and Developers on controls and secure implementation
- Works with QA/Testing on security and privacy verification
- Advises the Project Manager on risks, decisions, and escalation
- Helps the Product Manager understand constraints and trade-offs
- Communicates material risks and remediation status to Stakeholders

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign candidate persona responsibilities explicitly, or document that they are shared, before delivery work begins.
